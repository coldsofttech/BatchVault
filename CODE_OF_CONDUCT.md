# Code of conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Everyone taking part in this project is expected
to:

- Treat others with courtesy, whatever their background or experience.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Keep discussions on topic and free of harassment, personal attacks or
  discriminatory language.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break these
expectations, and may block repeat offenders. To report a problem, use the
private reporting route described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and mark it as a
conduct issue.
